Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

UnrealDevHarness Builds is a private tool used only by its owner, Curt Spiegelhalter.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it uses the Google Drive API to upload packaged game builds into one Drive folder the owner chose, to list the builds in that folder, and to delete builds older than the three newest. It does not read, change or delete any other files.

What it stores

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computers and build server. No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
